creatures.js harness

Standalone test page for creatures.js. It does not load scene3d.js, env3d.js or index.html — the 3D view below is a bare camera, one light and a flat water plane, only enough to exercise the module. The two things the module does are kept apart on this page on purpose, and must stay apart wherever either appears.

physics A. Stochastic driving of the modelled fish. Every random input is drawn from a stated distribution with a source. Seeded: the seed is in the URL (?cseed=), and the same seed gives the same ensemble.
