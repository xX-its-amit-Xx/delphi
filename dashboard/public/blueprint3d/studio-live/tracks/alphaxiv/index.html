Lean4Bio paper checks

We state laws of biology and of bio-based materials in Lean 4, a language in which a computer checks every step of a proof. Each day a watcher looks for new papers on arXiv and in journals, copies out the numbers they print, and asks one question: do these numbers sit inside the law or outside it? This page is for readers of alphaXiv and anyone else who wants to join in.

An independent research project. Not affiliated with alphaXiv or arXiv. A check is arithmetic on numbers copied from a paper. It is not a review and it certifies nothing.

Type the numbers from any paper. You get a verdict and a Lean theorem you can check yourself in a browser. Numbers that land outside a law go on the board.

2. One number short

Papers that are one missing number away from being checkable. Authors and readers can supply it.

3. Laws wanted

Regularities that papers report and nobody has stated in Lean. Each comes with a starter file.

4. A badge and a feed

Every checked paper gets a badge that links to its proof, a ready comment for its alphaXiv page, and an entry in a feed.
