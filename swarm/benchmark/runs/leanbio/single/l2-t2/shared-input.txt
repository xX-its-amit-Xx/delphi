THE CLAIM IN ENGLISH
The aerobic power a fish can supply is bounded above by the Fick product P_avail = eta * E * Q * (Ca - Cv), where eta is the mechanical efficiency, E the oxycaloric equivalent, Q the cardiac output and (Ca - Cv) the arterio-venous oxygen difference. Therefore if the power a sustained activity demands strictly exceeds P_avail, that activity cannot be sustained aerobically.

REQUIRED CONTENT. A statement that compiles but does not carry these is a failure:

  [5] a demanded power is named separately from the available power
  [6] the conclusion is a negation: the activity cannot be sustained
  [7] the arterio-venous difference is hypothesised non-negative, which is what stops the bound being vacuous

KNOWN TRAP: If the hypotheses are mutually contradictory the theorem is vacuously true and compiles. The witness declaration is what catches this, and `hypotheses_load_bearing` is what catches a conclusion that needs no hypotheses at all.
